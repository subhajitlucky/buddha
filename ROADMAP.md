### Primary Sources:
- Pali Canon (Tipitaka) - The earliest Buddhist texts

- Academic research papers on early Buddhism
- Museum archives and archaeological findings
- Monastic traditions and commentarial literature

## Enhanced Content Structure

### Life of Buddha:
- Detailed timeline with sources from Pali Canon and biographical texts
- Historical context of 6th century BCE India
- Archaeological evidence for key sites
- Relationships with family members and disciples

### Core Teachings:
- Four Noble Truths with references to specific suttas
- Noble Eightfold Path with detailed explanations from the Magga-vibhanga Sutta
- Advanced concepts like dependent origination and the three marks of existence
- Distinction between earliest teachings and later developments

### Meditation Practices:
- Foundational practices from the Satipatthana Sutta
- Loving-kindness meditation from the Metta Sutta
- Advanced practices including jhana and formless attainments
- Practical guidance with sources from commentarial literature

### Buddhist Texts:
- Complete overview of the Pali Canon (Tipitaka)
- Major Mahayana sutras with historical context
- Textual preservation methods across cultures
- Languages of Buddhist texts and translation history

This roadmap will guide the development of a world-class website dedicated to Buddha, ensuring we create an authentic, beautiful, and educational resource for people interested in Buddhism.
